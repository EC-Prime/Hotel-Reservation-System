Guest Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about your room, check-in, and booking.

What are the standard check-in and check-out times?

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM. Direct online bookings include guaranteed complimentary late checkout until 12:00 PM. Early arrival luggage storage and valet service are complimentary 24/7.

How do I check in?

Your digital room key will be ready 24 hours before you arrive. Use it to go straight to your room, or show your booking number at the front desk for help.

Can I cancel my booking?

You can cancel for free up to 48 hours before check-in. To change your booking, visit the Manage Booking page.

Is parking and airport transport included?

Parking is free for all guests. Guests booking a Signature Suite or Penthouse also get a ride to and from the airport.
